# Research Notes

## Initial Project Direction

Overall topic:
Vision-based gait recognition.

The goal of Project 1 is to establish a simple biometric identification
baseline that can later be expanded in Projects 2 and 3.

## Current Direction

Potential primary dataset:
CASIA-B

Potential baseline representation:
Gait Energy Image (GEI)

GEI is considered because it is simple, interpretable, and
appropriate for establishing a baseline before investigating stronger
representations later.
